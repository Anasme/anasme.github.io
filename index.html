About me

Hello there!

I’m Anas Asmi, a DevOps Engineer based in France, specializing in building scalable, secure, and resilient cloud infrastructures. With several years of experience in the field, I focus on designing solutions that combine automation, reliability, and security.

My expertise spans across multi-cloud architectures (AWS, Scaleway, GCP, Azure…), Kubernetes orchestration, CI/CD pipeline optimization, and comprehensive observability. I’ve architected and deployed production-grade infrastructures with high availability, automated scaling, and deep security enforcement including RBAC, centralized secret management, and automated TLS certificates.

I’m passionate about Infrastructure as Code, GitOps practices, and building frameworks that standardize and simplify complex deployments. Whether it’s designing a complete observability stack with Prometheus, ELK, and Jaeger, or refactoring CI/CD workflows for better maintainability, I strive to create systems that are both robust and elegant.

“Continuous improvement is not about perfection, it’s about progress.”

I believe in sharing knowledge and learning from the community. Technology evolves rapidly, and staying curious is key to building better solutions. I enjoy tackling challenging problems, whether they involve distributed systems, infrastructure optimization, or security hardening.

When I’m not working on infrastructure, I’m exploring new technologies or experimenting with tools that push the boundaries of what’s possible in the DevOps ecosystem.

Feel free to check out my GitHub or connect with me on LinkedIn.
